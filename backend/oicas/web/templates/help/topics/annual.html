A simple log for the yearly inventory. Every line records one item code, a quantity and a location for a period (P1–P4), an area (Warehouse or Production) and a section.

Logging an entry

  1. Open Inventory → Annual Inventory and hover a plant (Plant 1–4). Hover Warehouse or Production in the dropdown, then click Section 1 or Section 2. The counts show how many entries are logged there.
  2. The breadcrumb at the top (e.g. Annual Inventory › Plant 1 › Warehouse › Section 1) shows where you are; Back (or the first crumb) returns to the plants.
  3. Fill Item code, Quantity and Location (Remarks is optional). Quantity accepts numbers only (e.g. 12 or 1,250.5). Suggestions from earlier entries appear as you type: use ↑ ↓ and Enter to pick one.
  4. Click Enter or press Enter on the keyboard. The button stays grey until the required fields are filled.

The table

  • The search box looks in every column. Click a heading to sort.
  • Click ⋮ at the end of a row: Details shows who logged it and when and who edited it last; Edit changes the row in place (Enter saves, Esc cancels); Delete asks for confirmation.
  • Every add, edit and delete is also written to the Audit Trail.
This log is separate from Physical Counting and is not compared with Odoo yet. Quantities cannot be negative; enter 0 only if you checked and found none.
