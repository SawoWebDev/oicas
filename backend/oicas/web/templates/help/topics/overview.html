OICAS (Odoo Inventory Control & Analytics System) is SAWO's system for cycle counting and inventory accuracy. It replaces the Excel files we used before: AMC & ABC Analysis, the Inventory Masterlist & Cycle Count Tracker and the COUNTED LIST. It joins them into one connected system, so nothing has to be copied between files anymore.

What OICAS does for you

  • Reads stock from Odoo (erp.sawo.com): products, locations and on-hand quantities. It's read-only and never changes Odoo.
  • Classifies every item by demand: AMC (average monthly consumption), ABC class, movement class (Fast / Medium / Slow / Non Moving) and how often it must be counted (every 30, 90 or 180 days).
  • Plans the cycle counts: shows what's overdue or due and lets you create count plans and printable count sheets.
  • Records the physical counts per location (Warehouse / Production) and keeps every figure, correction and recount.
  • Compares counts with Odoo (physical − Odoo) and runs a review: investigate, recount, resolve.
  • Reports: a dashboard and 16 reports, each exportable to Excel and PDF.
  • Imports data that isn't in Odoo yet (items without a code, book balances, count lines, demand).

Live and demo

The badge at the top right of every page shows where you are:

  • LIVE · primary DB: real Odoo and real data. You log in with your ERP (Odoo) account.
  • DEMO · secondary DB: training mode with simulated Odoo and demo accounts. Nothing you do here affects live data.
Most important rule: a dash “–” means unknown / not counted, never zero. OICAS never treats something you didn't count as 0.
