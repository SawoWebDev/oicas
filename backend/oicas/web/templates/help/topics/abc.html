ABC ranks items within their family (RAW MAT, BOX, Metal Stamping, Pre-Assy, Stone, T&D, PIM, Control Unit, Wood, SF Wood, Excluded list) twice: by quantity of demand and by frequency of demand. The two letters together make the category.

Step by step

  1. Total demand per item = sum of the months in the period.
  2. Rank items in the family from the highest total to the lowest (ties by code).
  3. Demand % = item total ÷ family total. Cumulative % = running sum of demand % down the ranking, including the item itself.
  4. Quantity class: A if cumulative < 80 % · C if cumulative ≥ 95 % · otherwise B.
  5. Frequency = number of months with recorded demand; frequency % = frequency ÷ months in the period.
  6. Frequency class: A if > 80 % · C if ≤ 50 % · otherwise B.
  7. Category = quantity class + frequency class (e.g. A + B = AB).
  8. The category gives the action, classification and interval (see Movement Classification). An item with no demand in any month is always Non Moving.
Example
8RM04-341-1 in RAW MAT: cumulative % lands under 80 % → quantity A; demand in 6 of 11 months = 54.55 % → frequency B (between 50 % and 80 %) → category AB → CLOSE CONTROL, Fast Moving, every 30 days.

On the screen

  • Category distribution chart for the selected run.
  • Method box summarising the rules above.
  • ABC report: family, rank, code, total, demand %, cumulative % of items, cumulative % of demand, frequency, frequency %, quantity class, frequency class, category, declared type (MTS / MTO), action, classification. Filter by run, family, class or code.
Thresholds (80 / 95 / 80 / 50 %) are set in the rule set (System Configuration → Rule sets). A change creates a new version, and old runs keep their original version.
