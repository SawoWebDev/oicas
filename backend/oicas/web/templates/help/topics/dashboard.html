The dashboard is the management overview (the old PROGRESS-overall and PROGRESS-weekly sheets). Every figure is calculated live from count records, and you can click any tile to see the items behind it.

What's on the screen

FiltersMaterial section, ABC family, Plant location limit every figure on the page. The note on the right shows which approved analysis run gives the classification.
Total active itemsItems in the masterlist (tracked) after the filters.
Items countedItems with at least one finalized count, plus the completion % (counted ÷ items).
Items remainingNot counted yet.
Inventory accuracyCounted items whose latest count matched Odoo ÷ counted items that could be compared with Odoo.
Items with discrepancyLatest count differs from Odoo, and the % of counted items.
Overdue countsOverdue or due today, plus how many are due in the next 7 days.
Overall progress by movement classThe PROGRESS-overall table: # of items, # counted, # of discrepancy, counted with no Odoo balance, count completion, inventory accuracy, overall accuracy and discrepancy rate for Fast / Medium / Slow / Non Moving and the total. Click a class name to open those items.
Completion & accuracy chartThe same KPIs as bars and lines per class.
Weekly progressLast 8 weeks (Monday–Sunday): items counted, discrepancies and accuracy %. Full table in report 7.
ABC category distributionHow many items are in each category (AA … CC).
Progress by plant locationItems, counted and a completion bar per plant (1, 2, 3, 4, 1 & 3 …).
Counts by person (30 days)Finalized item counts per counter.
Odoo sync & data qualityLatest snapshot (current or stale), last sync, and the most common open data-quality issues.
Recent activityThe latest actions (counts, finalizations, imports…).

Buttons

Plan counts opens planning filtered to overdue items. New Count opens Physical Counting.

Example
Fast Moving: 821 items, 488 counted → completion 488 ÷ 821 = 59.44 %. Of those, 78 had no Odoo balance to compare, so 410 could be verified and 353 differed: accuracy = (410 − 353) ÷ 410 = 13.90 %; discrepancy rate = 353 ÷ 410 = 86.10 %.
