OICAS (Odoo Inventory Control & Analytics System) is SAWO's cycle-count, inventory-accuracy and inventory-analysis system. It reads stock from Odoo and never changes it.

  1. Live (green LIVE badge): enter your ERP (erp.sawo.com) username/email and password, then press Log in with Odoo. Odoo checks your password; OICAS doesn't store it.
  2. Demo (yellow banner): use admin, manager, counter or viewer with password demo. Demo data never affects live data.
  3. After 5 wrong attempts, wait 15 minutes.
  4. If Odoo is unreachable, an administrator can use the Emergency administrator login (break-glass account), if one is configured.

Your role (viewer, counter, manager or admin) comes from your Odoo groups. After you sign in, press F1 on any page, or open Help Center in the sidebar, for the full guide.
